# Team Training venue — Bell94 / Scottsdale Sports Complex
Revision 1.2 • 19 October 2026
This adds the owner's venue direction to the training revision. Bell94 is the primary visual reference; Scottsdale Sports Complex is an acceptable alternate. Both describe the organized team-training location.

## Locked direction
The user requested a training field that looks like Bell94 or Scottsdale Sports Complex. Keep the established 2D retro game style, closer training camera, recognizable player sprites and half-field tactical session.

Visual ingredients:
- A broad, carefully maintained green grass sports field with quiet mowing texture.
- Tall slim floodlight poles outside field boundaries.
- Open neighboring fields and modest sideline facilities.
- Tan paths, desert planting and a low mountain backdrop.
- A shaded pavilion / rest area and benches kept outside the ball/player action.
- A practical local soccer-complex atmosphere.

The city documents groomed Bermuda-grass fields, goals/nets, field lighting, paths and patio facilities. Bell94's designer also documents a shaded plaza and desert landscaping. Building shape, precise object placement, camera and lighting in our illustration are original artistic interpretation.
