5. PORTRAIT SCALING AND TOUCH BEHAVIOR

These are tall portrait layouts. Do not stretch them into the landscape match viewport.

• Use a portrait-oriented UI scene or screen.
• Scale uniformly from each image’s actual dimensions.
• Use a shared coordinate transform for the image, overlays, and hit areas.
• Define panel anchors in normalized image coordinates.
• Recalculate layout on resize and orientation changes.
• Respect iPhone safe areas and browser toolbars.
• Prioritize readable text over fitting the entire image on one screen.
• Allow vertical scrolling when needed.
• In landscape, retain a readable centered portrait layout with scrolling or surrounding margins.
• Keep text within panel bounds and handle long player/team names.
• Add accessible touch-sized hit areas without overlapping neighboring controls.

Do not automatically force device rotation merely to view these menus.

6. REQUIRED DATA BINDINGS

Connect both screens to the same authoritative data:

• Player appearance → saved character customization.
• Overall and attributes → persistent player attributes and position-weighted overall.
• Form → recent performance trend.
• Energy → readiness system.
• Weekly activities → schedule and completion records.
• Match statistics → finalized match-event aggregates.
• Match rating → role-specific evaluation.
• Highlights → recorded significant events.
• Development → contribution and settlement ledgers.
• Coach feedback → evidence-based recommendation rules.

Keep historical match reports tied to the saved match snapshot. A later attribute increase, team transfer, or name change must not silently rewrite historical performance data.

Current My Player information should reflect current career state.
