RESEARCH REGISTER — accessed 15 October 2026
These sources inform design principles, not proof of game efficacy. Proposed timings, story structures and emotional states are original product decisions. This package makes no claim that a digital minigame transfers to real-world mastery.

1. Center for Self-Determination Theory — The Theory
https://selfdeterminationtheory.org/the-theory/
Supports autonomy, competence and relatedness as motivation concepts. Application: meaningful choice, visible learning and relationships; not a clinical emotion score.

2. CDC — School Connectedness Helps Students Thrive
https://www.cdc.gov/youth-behavior/school-connectedness/index.html
Supports belonging through feeling valued and supported by adults and peers. Application: individual relationships, not winning as the condition for belonging.

3. StopBullying.gov — The Roles Kids Play in Bullying
https://www.stopbullying.gov/bullying/roles-kids-play
Describes changing participation, bystander and defender roles. Application: varied peer behavior, adult support and no fixed villain label.

4. StopBullying.gov — Bystanders to Bullying
https://www.stopbullying.gov/prevention/bystanders-to-bullying
Supports meaningful peer support. Application: private and public supportive scenes independent of soccer results.

5. FIFA Training Centre / Diogo Coutinho — Small-sided games, part 1
https://www.fifatrainingcentre.com/en/environment/science-explained/high-performance/train/diogo-coutinho-on-small-sided-games-part1.php
Describes manipulating player count, space, instructions, targets, scoring and duration. Application: vary soccer tasks; does not validate violent playground behavior or digital skill transfer.

6. inkle — So what is 80 Days?
https://www.inklestudios.com/2014/04/15/so-what-is-eighty-days
Developer describes remembered choices and good/bad consequences. Application: explicit memories and delayed callbacks.

7. inkle — Writing web-based interactive fiction with ink
https://www.inklestudios.com/ink/web-tutorial/
Shows structured narrative authoring and engine integration. Reference only; do not introduce ink or replace the project's current narrative stack without inspecting its needs.

Research limitation: this is a selected primary-source design synthesis, not a systematic literature review, coaching approval, psychological assessment or clinical intervention. No real children's data is required.
