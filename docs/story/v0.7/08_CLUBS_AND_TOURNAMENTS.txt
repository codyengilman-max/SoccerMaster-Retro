SIX CLUBS
FC Batavia: Coach Code, personal attention, continuity, learning and a team growing together. Early modest team results can coexist with player improvement. Do not secretly weaken opponents or guarantee future championships.
Royal Salt Academy: established pathway and competitive standards; depth creates uncertainty about individual opportunities.
Valley Rising: visibility and ambitious events; expectations and reputation create pressure.
Desert Tuzon: competitive intensity and group identity; fitting an approach matters. No ethnic stereotype or blanket misconduct label.
Sol Vista: structure and an established environment; individual goals can conflict with organizational priorities.
Next Horizon: prestigious tournament access, travel, visibility and self-confidence bordering on entitlement. Opportunities are real within the fiction; superiority must be tested on the field. Talented, friendly and insecure players can coexist. The club is not a universal villain.
All clubs have credible strengths, limits and individual variation. No club badge grants a hidden stat multiplier or transfer penalty.

LEAGUE PARTICIPATION
For the initial fictional schedule, model Next Horizon as NOT ENTERED in the local league. This is the implementation default for the user's tournament-focused direction; it can later be changed by an explicit competition configuration. Do not infer zero points, a bottom ranking, forfeits or missed fixtures. Generate league fixtures and table rows solely from actual season entries. Do not assume the remaining five clubs are all entered; their entries require an actual schedule.
Next Horizon may meet Batavia at configured tournaments or friendlies. Schoolyard matches are not official club matches. Its reputation does not create a game result or count toward standings.

TWO MARQUEE TOURNAMENTS
Pacific Wave Cup and Lone Star International Cup: U12 playable introduction. No event dates, qualification formula, format, roster size, travel cost or results have been approved here. Leave those data unconfigured, not guessed. Next Horizon receives an invitation for each eligible configured season/event independent of league participation or record. This does not imply attendance at every age group forever or entry into an undefined event edition.
U11 reference scene is aspiration/established standing-invitation policy, not an accepted U11 tournament entry. A confirmed invitation for an event edition requires that edition and age category exist. If U12 progression is not implemented, foreshadow only and label future content honestly.
Other clubs' entry routes are UNCONFIGURED. Do not silently choose league qualification, deny entry, promise access or award it based on friendship. Until a supported entry route is authored, entry UI says 'Entry route not yet configured'; week-one play remains available and has no dependency on tournament entry.

STATE AND OWNERSHIP
Separate competitionId, editionId, seasonId, ageCategory, clubId and teamId; a club may have several teams. Invitation is club-level or team-level according to explicit edition data; never guess which team it covers. Single-team prototype may map club to one explicit team.
Invitation states: unissued -> issued -> accepted | declined | expired. An explicit cancellation may close an accepted invitation under documented event policy; no hidden revocation from league losses. Registration states: pending -> confirmed | rejected | cancelled. Individual selection: undecided -> selected | not_selected, scoped to a confirmed team entry and a versioned selection evaluation. Participation and match result are separate facts.
Standing invitation policy creates one issued record per (editionId, ageCategory, clubId, invitationType), transactionally. League record is not an input. Resubmitting changes no state. Acceptance requires correct invitation, eligible registered team mapping, explicit authorized action and published prerequisites; invitation alone never means registration complete or travel selected.
Transfer is not implemented by this addition. Future transfers change current membership through existing authority and do not rewrite historical team-at-event. A player joining Next Horizon does not automatically inherit a travel place or replay invitation rewards. No XP, badge or relationship points for merely processing an invitation. Claims about recruiting must distinguish interest, invitation, offer, registration and actual selection.
