IMPLEMENTATION GATES
1. Identity: all named surfaces use chosen name and correct pronouns; renaming preserves IDs, history and awards. Boy/girl with identical input/seed have equal gameplay outcomes. International names render as text; unsafe tokens/oversized imports rejected.
2. Crossbar: bar/post/net/ground distinguishable; fast ball cannot tunnel through bar; one shot one score; warm-up excluded; ties/skip/reload truthful. Regular match shots still work.
3. Controls: no Pass/Shoot buttons; dribble and kick input unchanged; orientation/background cancels or resumes gesture safely, never firing stale kick.
4. Narrative: private information cannot appear in school; unknown/skipped match never becomes a loss; rivalry uses actual opponent; all optional declines proceed.
5. Emotional firewall: changing every emotional state with identical match seed/input leaves physics, stats, rankings, growth and eligibility unchanged. Essential support/core training remain available at every state.
6. Training: one arrival per real session; retries/reloads create no new scene or rewards. Focus initializes once and locks only at defined boundary.
7. Persistence: race two tabs at scene selection and choice submission; only one occurrence/choice/effect wins. Simulate quota failure, aborted transaction and migration failure. Recover prior valid state without duplicate reward.
8. Commitments: no invitation becomes missed; cancelled/missed/closed commitment rejects late original completion. Repair requires confirmation and active bound session. Early/foreign/replayed completions fail. Completion vs advance race has one winner. One miss plus repair never profits in trust.
9. Content: all scenes have exits; no missing steps/tokens/assets; actor/role restrictions truthful; neutral fallbacks for absent evidence. No endless adversity loop.
10. Runtime: browser desktop and touch emulation plus actual owner iPhone test; pause, rotation, small landscape layout, sound controls, readable text, skip detail. Never equate emulation with device acceptance.
11. Deployment: test exact built commit in preview; report URL and version; no production merge under this package.
12. Existing regressions: saves, weekly growth, training, tactical moments, keeper/outfield roles, matches and current gameplay repairs.
