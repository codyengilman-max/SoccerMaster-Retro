TEAM TRAINING — Give Them an Option

Code positions a defender between two teammates.

CODE: “Call for it.”

SAM: “HERE!”

CODE: “Louder.”

SAM: “HERE!”

CODE: “Did the defender disappear?”

Sam steps sideways.

CODE: “THERE. Now your teammate can actually use you.”

Choose your question:

* “How far should I move?”
* “What if the defender follows?”
* “What do I do after I pass?”

If “How far”:

CODE: “Far enough to open the lane. Close enough to help. Keep checking—you don’t get a permanent parking space.”

If “Defender follows”:

CODE: “Then you’ve moved a defender. Look at what opened for somebody else.”

If “After I pass”:

CODE: “Find your next job. Support underneath, move beyond, or hold useful space. Don’t admire the pass.”

In a brief scripted demonstration, Blake passes, stays put, then throws both hands up when the return ball doesn’t come.

CODE: “Blake. You passed, then watched. Give them an angle.”

BLAKE: “I was open.”

CODE: “You were calling. That’s different. Move so they can find you.”

Blake drops both hands and moves. Code immediately turns back to the drill.

The correction is over. Blake’s expression says otherwise.

Launch mandatory tactical training.

Outfield focus: Move out of a defender’s cover shadow, create supporting angles, and recognize when movement opens space for another teammate.

Goalkeeper focus: Adjust the supporting position behind possession and offer a usable return pass without abandoning appropriate defensive positioning.

Evaluate decisions using the actual field situation.

After a strong decision:

CODE: “YES! You made your teammate’s next decision easier. That’s good soccer.”

After a sound decision with poor execution:

CODE: “The option was right. Now clean up the execution.”

After a poor decision:

CODE: “Freeze. Can the player on the ball actually reach you?”

Award attributes from actual team-training performance.
