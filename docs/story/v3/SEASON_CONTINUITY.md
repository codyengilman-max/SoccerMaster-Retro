## Competition
Twelve league games, as scheduled above. Tournaments are Weeks 9 and 12, three weeks apart.
Each tournament: eight teams, two groups of four; each group has two league and two out-of-state teams. Three group games; group winners directly reach the final. Two games Saturday, third Sunday, then final: three or four games per team.
Scoring: win 6, draw 3, loss 0; add scored goals up to 3, plus 1 clean-sheet point. Maximum 10 per match; 0–0 draw gives 4 each. Keep the previously supplied tournament tiebreak rules.
Tournament results change separate overall team strength ranking, displayed in the standings portfolio, never league points.
State Cup: actual league top three plus invited Next Level from outside the league. Next Horizon is Blake's separate club and must qualify normally. Save the draw once.
Preserve all finalist/nonfinalist endings. A season ends after its closing story, not at the final whistle.

## Continuity safeguards
Blake is on the original team through League 6 and only on Next Horizon afterward. Alex's recruiting/staying arc remains separate.
Pacific Wave Cup and Lone Star International Cup are the established fictional names for the future bigger events. Blake's “only team in the state” boast is a character claim unless verified in the fictional world.
Financial constraints never reduce ability directly. No invented scholarship or automatic paid training fixes Mom's Week 12 problem.
Recovery uses actual minutes, health and fatigue. The adjusted home plan remains achievable and required. No automatic energy reset between tournament matches.
