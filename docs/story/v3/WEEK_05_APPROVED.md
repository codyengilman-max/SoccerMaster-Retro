## TEAM TRAINING — Make Them Decide

Code rolls a ball into the practice area.

**CODE:** “HEADS UP! The grass isn’t going anywhere.”

**SAM:** “Neither are we, apparently.”

**CODE:** “Heard that. Show me otherwise.”

Code gathers the players around the half-field setup.

**CODE:** “Find the space. Use it. When someone comes to stop you, see what they’ve left.”

**PLAYER:** “So dribble until someone comes?”

**CODE:** “If the pass is already there, use it. Read the field. Don’t memorize an answer.”

Launch the mandatory tactical training activity.

**Outfield focus:** Recognize open space, draw pressure when appropriate, and identify the resulting passing option. Look for a switch when the defence shifts and the route is available.

**Goalkeeper focus:** Scan the press, identify available support, and choose distribution appropriate to the pressure and passing lanes.

Questions and evaluations come from the actual training situation.

**After a strong decision:**

**CODE:** “YES! You saw it before it happened. That’s the game!”

**After a sound decision with poor execution:**

**CODE:** “Right idea. Now give the pass the weight it needs. Again.”

**After a poor decision:**

**CODE:** “Freeze. Where was the pressure? Show me what you missed.”

Award team-training attributes from actual session performance.
