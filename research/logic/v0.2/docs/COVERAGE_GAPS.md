# Coverage gaps

The 40 original seeds remain available and unchanged in the archive and raw
catalog fields. Corrections are separate override fields.

Not yet covered deeply enough for a complete engine:

- Scanning before receiving, deception, recognition delay and communication.
- Ball shielding, first-touch direction, recovery touches and ball momentum.
- Shot selection, weak-foot finishes, rebounds and keeper saves.
- Aerial contests, bouncing balls and weather/surface effects if supported.
- Kickoffs, penalties, direct/indirect free kicks, goal-kick rules and substitutions.
- Advantage, fouls, cards, injuries and numerical imbalance if included in scope.
- Opponent adaptation, set-play deception and role changes.
- Women's/youth/non-European empirical samples and ability-stratified calibration.
- Team/season-specific manager dossiers, failure cases and training methodologies.
- Continuous tracking or properly bounded alternatives for off-ball valuation.

No item here is claimed implemented or researched to completion. A broader
catalog must expand rather than silently repurpose the preserved original IDs.
