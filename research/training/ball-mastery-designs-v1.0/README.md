# SoccerMaster Ball Mastery demonstration designs

14 original four-step technical storyboards, with 28 right/left SVG boards and 14 right-led PNG previews. Start with index.html or the boards folder. designs.json contains teaching beats and contact labels; DEVIN_DEMONSTRATION_DESIGN.md explains implementation. RESEARCH.md identifies nine sources and evidence limits.

Eight designs are based on source text; six require additional mechanics or naming review. All 14 still need coach and finished-media review. No motion-captured footage, complete sprite animation, video verification or coach approval is claimed. The drawings show ball routes and contact labels, not biomechanically complete human poses. Do not load them as approved player lessons.

This is an add-on, not a replacement for research expansion v2. No source videos, source PDFs or third-party logos are bundled. No live game or scoring changes are made.
