# Demonstration research

Nine references were inspected for source text or listed patterns. No source video was downloaded, watched frame-by-frame or copied. Coordinates, timings and rendering choices are original design proposals, not measured biomechanics.

## D1 — C. P. de Vera — YDP Ball Mastery

https://www.sportsessionplanner.com/s/iGiKh/YDP---Ball-Mastery-.html

Named sections describe sole taps, inside pulls, tap-tap-roll and inside squish. Text reviewed, not video frames.

## D2 — Coerver — Learn football skills like Messi

https://www.coervercoaching.com/blog/learn-football-skills-like-messi/

Side-step sequence and contextual principles for changes of direction and stop/start. Text reviewed.

## D3 — FourFourTwo / Coerver — Ball mastery warm-up Part 1

https://www.fourfourtwo.com/performance/training/video-ball-mastery-warm-part-1

Inside-foot transfers and two-transfer/sole-roll combination. Article calls inside contacts toe taps. Published timestamps 0:00–0:49 and 1:09–1:35 are source-provided, not independently frame-verified.

## D4 — Sikana — Quickly Turn the Ball

https://www.sikana.tv/en/sport/football/soccer-skills-quickly-turn-the-ball

Transcript explains an inside-foot quick turn and supporting-foot orientation. Does not verify an outside-cut demonstration.

## D5 — Sikana — The Drag-Back

https://www.sikana.tv/en/sport/football/soccer-skills-the-drag-back

Transcript specifies sole pull followed by same-foot inside exit in a V path. Mapping to Coerver’s separate V label remains unresolved.

## D6 — Coaching American Soccer — First Fakes and Feints

https://coachingamericansoccer.com/intermediate-soccer-skills/soccer-first-fakes-and-feints/

Text contains Cruyff cut-back and stop/go. Its Roll Over is a feint with an outside exit, not proof of a simple lateral sole-roll pattern.

## D8 — Coerver session plan

https://www.coervercoaching.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/03/COERVER-SESSION-90-min-3-march.pdf

## D9 — The FA — Skill: what does it actually look like?

https://thefa.com/bootroom/resources/coaching/skill-what-does-it-actually-look-like

Distinguishes body, ball and decision-making; supports contextual learning rather than treating a move as an automatic correct answer.

## Important corrections

Use Sole taps for top-of-ball contacts and Inside-foot shuffles for inside transfers. Do not treat Roll Over feint and basic lateral sole roll as identical. Keep V pull-and-exit mapping unresolved. Outside cut, dribble cut and pull/push need stronger mechanics evidence before animation. Do not infer that a move is the correct tactical choice merely because its technique is demonstrated.
